The serving-size trap & six guides

The oldest trick on a packet: a tiny “serving” that makes the numbers look small. The pack is the serving that matters.

One biscuit is 5g of sugar. The pack is 25g.

The label’s per-serving column uses the smallest serving the maker can justify. Multiply by what you actually eat — that is the number that counts.

Rule of thumb: if you finish the pack, count the whole pack.
